# Figure 1 | Cross-cohort prediction and overlap of selected gut microbial species

**A,** Strictly nested leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) validation for colorectal adenoma (CRA), colorectal cancer (CRC) and inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). Each row is labelled by the held-out INSDC BioProject accession. Points show held-out-project ROC-AUC estimates and horizontal lines show stratified bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. The dashed vertical line marks AUC = 0.5. The I² printed beside each disease heading is an exploratory heterogeneity estimate on the logit-AUC scale. Standard errors were approximated from transformed bootstrap 95% CI widths; correlations induced by overlapping LODO training datasets were not modelled. These annotations are not confirmatory inference. All feature filtering, ranking, correlation filtering, selection of the feature number (*K*), standardization and model fitting were restricted to the remaining training projects.

**B,** Between-project heterogeneity of strictly nested LODO AUC. Points show individual held-out projects, thin lines span the minimum to maximum, thick translucent lines show the interquartile range and diamonds mark medians. Source Data Figure 1B additionally provides sample SD (n−1 denominator) and mean AUC.

**C,** Observed intersections of selected species sets (bars; labels above bars show observed/expected fold enrichment, ×) and mean intersections expected under 100,000 fixed-size random draws (peach points). Each set was sampled independently and uniformly without replacement from its own detectable-species background: CRC/CRA, 1,813 species; IBD, 1,357; IBS, 465. Selected-set sizes remained 300, 300 and 81, respectively. CR denotes the unified CRC/CRA set. The IBS set comprises the union of species ranked in the Top 50 by random forest feature importance in the training model of at least one of five stratified cross-validation folds within the 465-species filtered Mars_2020 background. Species-level *q* values and absolute log2 fold changes were not thresholded for IBS membership. IBS is represented by one cohort. Asterisks and dark bars indicate BH-adjusted *q* < 0.05 together with an observed count above the 95th percentile of the null distribution; these indicate enrichment of set intersections, not significance of individual species. CRC/CRA–IBD: observed = 66, expected = 45.42, observed/expected = 1.45, *q* = 0.001360; CRC/CRA–IBS: observed = 18, expected = 13.34, observed/expected = 1.35, *q* = 0.103059; IBD–IBS: observed = 25, expected = 17.56, observed/expected = 1.42, *q* = 0.040626; CRC/CRA–IBD–IBS: observed = 8, expected = 2.91, observed/expected = 2.75, *q* = 0.015900. Empirical upper-tail *p* values used the plus-one correction and were adjusted across all four comparisons by the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure. The random seed was 20260903.

**D,** Pairwise species-overlap matrix. Diagonal cells show set sizes and off-diagonal cells show shared-species counts. Rows and columns are labelled CR (CRC/CRA), IBD and IBS (Mars_2020). Inferential statistics are shown in C and are not repeated in D.

CRA, colorectal adenoma; CRC, colorectal cancer; IBD, inflammatory bowel disease; IBS, irritable bowel syndrome; LODO, leave-one-dataset-out; ROC-AUC, area under the receiver-operating-characteristic curve; BH, Benjamini–Hochberg.
